Ships are expensive. Space is dangerous.

Your decisions
are questionable.

Engines stop. Guns go silent. Hulls come apart. A ship can lose the fight long before it’s gone.

Command a persistent fleet through a war where the damage matters. Crews evacuate. Rescue ships latch on under fire. Getting home in one piece is a nice idea. Getting home at all will do.

A few ways this can go.
