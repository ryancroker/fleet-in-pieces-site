What is Fleet in Pieces?

A space war.
A fleet worth keeping.

A physics-heavy strategy game about commanding ships that have real machinery, carrying people and supplies you cannot afford to waste.

You command the navy. Find out what is out there, decide where your fleet is needed, and live with what comes back.

The goal

Depth you can see and understand.

A damaged engine should change how a ship moves. A lost sensor should change what you know. A tanker arriving should let a stranded force move again.

That is the idea: your orders are simple; the ships and the situation give them depth. Pause to issue orders, then watch the fleet carry them out with the hardware, information and supplies it actually has.

The command loop

The battle is only part of it.

  1. 01 / FIND

    Build a picture.

    Survey a route. Read your sensor contacts. Decide what needs defending.

  2. 02 / COMMIT

    Choose your force.

    Bring the right weapons, screens and support. Keep a way out.

  3. 03 / RECOVER

    Bring them home.

    Withdraw damaged ships. Recover aircraft. Go back for the survivors.

  4. 04 / SUSTAIN

    Prepare the next fleet.

    Refuel, repair and replace what you lost with the resources you have.

What you command

A navy inside a working world.

Direct the operation.

Move formations, set targets, assign wings and choose when to jump, repair, board or recover survivors.

Protect what sustains it.

Civilian government, industry and merchants have their own activity. Survey opportunities and decide where to promise naval protection.

Choose what comes next.

Ships, research and emergency supplies compete for finite funding. A replacement needs materials, trained people and a yard that can build it.
