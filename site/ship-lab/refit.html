    Quarters & life support

    Life support uses power and component condition in this browser. Physical bunk damage, detached compartments and atmosphere remain native-game checks.

    A complement is a plan, not people already aboard. In the game, qualified personnel must be assigned from the existing roster.

    What can I change in this first pass?

    Independent weapons, projectors, reactors, drives and other exported equipment packages can be removed and reinstalled at their original mounts. A turret includes its bearing and barrel. Assemblies containing unclassified feed, cooling or service machinery stay fixed until their native contracts distinguish those services. Hull-integrated equipment remains fixed. You cannot turn an empty socket into an extra reactor or a different weapon without a matching article contract.

    Shape, material mass, equipment ratings and dependency chains come from the selected published edition. Module removal changes the drawn parts, rated demand, generation and available systems. It does not create an interchangeable space budget. Shipyard cost, structural clearance, ammunition compatibility, heat, crew and native construction admission remain separate.

    Damage and stored supplies survive removing and reinstalling equipment. Restore stock explicitly resets both the fit and this local scenario. KESTREL’s article editor and current KITE’s article editor retain their existing alternatives.
