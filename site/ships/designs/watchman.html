WATCHMAN.

WATCHMAN is a medium naval sensor frigate with four exposed receiver panels, a protected correlation computer, a separate communications mast, two point-defense guns and four shields. It builds the shared passive picture while stationary; active close mine search is a separate mode.

TradeoffsDesign notes
The receiver panels and protected processing support a stationary passive observation role. Building the picture takes time and constrains movement; its two defensive guns do not turn it into a general-purpose warship.Registrar design interpretation of the documented architecture; not a new capability.
