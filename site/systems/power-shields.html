How it works

Can everything run at once?

Weapons, protection and specialist equipment compete for finite power.

Protection has hardware behind it

Shield coverage depends on the ship’s fitted emitters and their supplied power. Surviving hull and working protection are different things; an emitter or a power shortage can leave part of a ship exposed.

A useful ship can have an awkward tradeoff

WATCHMAN’s active search can compete with its shields and defensive guns for power. That is a reason to bring escorts and choose when to search. Carrier operations also need clear routes through their own protection for departing and returning craft.

Your decision

Run the search you need now, or preserve more power for protection while an escort moves into place?
