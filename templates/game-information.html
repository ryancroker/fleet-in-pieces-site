The game

A hit should
change the plan.

Ships are working machines. What breaks, what runs out, and who survives all shape the next decision.

01

Damage changes the fight.

Lose engines, guns or shields and face a different problem. A damaged ship can still be dangerous.

02

The fleet needs support.

Fuel, ammunition, power and repair supplies are finite. Winning is only part of keeping ships alive.

03

Position matters.

Thrust, momentum and jump preparation shape how you approach a fight—and how you get out.

04

There’s more out there.

Surveys, discoveries and research are an active development focus, giving you new reasons to explore.
